# Topgrading Competencies Reference

## Rating Scale

| Rating | Label | Description |
|--------|-------|-------------|
| 4 | Excellent | Top 10% performer in this competency |
| 3 | Very Good | Above average, meets high standards |
| 2 | Good | Acceptable, meets basic requirements |
| 1 | Weak | Below expectations, needs development |

## Ease of Change Scale

| Color | Label | Description |
|-------|-------|-------------|
| RED | Difficult | Deep-seated trait, very hard to change |
| YELLOW | Hard But Doable | Can be developed with significant effort |
| GREEN | Easy | Trainable skill, can be improved quickly |

## Competency Selection Guidelines

### For Individual Contributor Roles
Select 15-20 competencies:
- Up to 3 from Sales Knowledge/Skills (mandatory for sales roles)
- Up to 3 from Behavioral & Intellectual categories
- At least 1 from Keboola-Specific category
- At least 1 from Motivational category

### For Managerial Roles
Select 15-20 competencies:
- All Individual Contributor guidelines PLUS:
- At least 3 from Managerial categories (mandatory)

### Setting Minimum Acceptable Ratings (MAR)
- Critical competencies: MAR = 3 or 4
- Important competencies: MAR = 2 or 3
- Nice-to-have competencies: MAR = 1 or 2

### Prioritizing by Ease of Change
- **RED (Difficult)**: Prioritize highly - these won't change
- **YELLOW (Hard But Doable)**: Consider carefully - development possible
- **GREEN (Easy)**: Lower priority - can be trained
