# Job Scorecard Examples

## Scorecard Structure

Every Topgrading Job Scorecard contains these sections:

1. **Role Information** - Title, team, type (individual/managerial), reporting structure
2. **Mission** - Primary purpose of the role (1-2 sentences)
3. **Key Accountabilities** - 10-15 measurable outcomes with specific metrics
4. **Competencies** - 15-20 competencies with Minimum Acceptable Ratings
5. **Compensation** - Base, commission/bonus, OTE range

## Tips for Creating Effective Scorecards

### Mission Statement
- Keep it to 1-2 sentences
- Make it specific and measurable
- Include timeframe when relevant
- Focus on the "why" of the role

### Key Accountabilities
- Use SMART criteria (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound)
- Include 10-15 accountabilities
- Mix short-term and long-term objectives
- Make metrics objectively verifiable
- Cover all critical areas of the role

### Competencies
- Select 15-20 competencies
- Set realistic Minimum Acceptable Ratings
- Balance "must-have" (MAR 3-4) vs "nice-to-have" (MAR 1-2)
- Consider "ease of change" when setting MARs
- Include role-specific competencies (sales, managerial, etc.)
