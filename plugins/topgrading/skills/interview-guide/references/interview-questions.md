# Topgrading Interview Questions Reference

## Interview Structure

The Topgrading Interview follows a **chronological career history** format, starting from education and progressing through each job to the present.

### Estimated Timing
- **Introduction & Rapport**: 10-15 minutes
- **Education Section**: 15-20 minutes
- **Career History** (per job): 15-30 minutes each
- **Closing**: 10-15 minutes
- **Total**: 2-4 hours depending on experience level

---

## The Five Core Questions (Per Job)

For EACH position in the candidate's career history, ask these five questions:

### 1. What were you hired to do?
**Purpose**: Understand role expectations and scope

Follow-ups:
- What was your title?
- What were your key responsibilities?
- What results were you expected to achieve?
- How was success measured?

### 2. What accomplishments are you most proud of?
**Purpose**: Identify strengths and achievements

Follow-ups:
- What specific results did you achieve?
- How did you accomplish that?
- What was your specific contribution vs. the team's?
- How did that compare to expectations?

### 3. What were some low points during that job?
**Purpose**: Uncover challenges, mistakes, and learning

Follow-ups:
- What happened?
- What was your role in that situation?
- What would you do differently?
- What did you learn from that experience?

### 4. Who were the people you worked with?
**Purpose**: Understand working relationships and gather future reference info

Questions for each manager:
- What was your manager's name?
- What was their title?
- What would they say were your strengths?
- What would they say were areas for improvement?
- How would they rate your overall performance?
- **TORC**: When we speak with them, what do you think they'll tell us?

### 5. Why did you leave that job?
**Purpose**: Understand career decisions and motivations

Follow-ups:
- Was it your decision or the company's?
- What factors influenced your decision?
- What were you looking for in your next role?
- Any regrets about that decision?

## 18 Follow-Up Probing Questions

Use these throughout the interview to dig deeper:

### Clarifying Questions
1. **"What did that look like?"** - Get concrete examples
2. **"Will you please give me a specific example?"** - Move from general to specific
3. **"Help me understand..."** - Invite elaboration
4. **"Can you walk me through that?"** - Get step-by-step details

### Impact Questions
5. **"What was the result?"** - Focus on outcomes
6. **"How did you measure success?"** - Understand metrics
7. **"What was the business impact?"** - Quantify value
8. **"How did that compare to expectations?"** - Context for performance

### Challenge Questions
9. **"What challenges did you face?"** - Identify obstacles
10. **"What could you have done differently?"** - Self-awareness check
11. **"What mistakes did you make?"** - Probe for honesty
12. **"What would you change if you could do it over?"** - Learning orientation

### Relationship Questions
13. **"How did others respond to that?"** - Team dynamics
14. **"Who else was involved?"** - Collaboration style
15. **"How did your manager react?"** - Authority relationships
16. **"What feedback did you receive?"** - Openness to input

### Motivation Questions
17. **"What motivated you in that situation?"** - Understand drivers
18. **"Why was that important to you?"** - Values clarification

## Education Section Questions

### High School
- Where did you go to high school?
- What were your favorite subjects? Why?
- Were you involved in any activities or sports?
- What were your grades like?
- Any jobs during high school?
- Who were the biggest influences on you during this time?

### College/University
- Where did you go to college? Why did you choose that school?
- What was your major? Why?
- How did you decide on that field of study?
- What were your grades? Are you satisfied with them?
- How did you pay for your education?
- Any jobs during college? What did you learn from them?
- What activities were you involved in?
- What were your career aspirations at graduation?

### Graduate School (if applicable)
- Why did you pursue graduate education?
- How did you choose your program?
- What was your research focus?
- How did this shape your career direction?

## TORC (Threat of Reference Check) Setup

Introduce TORC early in the interview:

**Script**:
> "As part of our process, we'll ask you to arrange reference calls with your past managers. We find this helps us understand your full professional history and allows candidates to present themselves most accurately. When we get to each job, I'll ask you what you think your manager would say about you if we spoke with them."

### TORC Questions Per Job
- What would [manager name] say were your biggest strengths?
- What areas for improvement would they mention?
- How would they rate your overall performance on a 1-10 scale?
- Is there anything they might say that you'd want to provide context for?
- Are you comfortable arranging a call with them for us?

## Competency-Based Behavioral Questions

### For Sales Competencies
- **Closing Ability**: Tell me about a complex deal you closed. What was your approach?
- **Objection Handling**: Describe a time when a customer had significant concerns. How did you address them?
- **Territory Knowledge**: How did you develop your understanding of your territory/market?

### For Leadership Competencies
- **Selecting A Players**: Walk me through your hiring process. How do you identify top talent?
- **Developing Others**: Tell me about someone you developed. What was your approach?
- **Performance Management**: How have you handled underperformers?

### For Intellectual Competencies
- **Strategic Thinking**: Describe a strategy you developed. How did you approach it?
- **Problem Solving**: Tell me about a complex problem you solved. Walk me through your process.
- **Decision Making**: Describe a difficult decision you made with incomplete information.

### For Interpersonal Competencies
- **Communication**: Give me an example of presenting complex information to a non-technical audience.
- **Conflict Resolution**: Tell me about a conflict with a colleague. How did you handle it?
- **Collaboration**: Describe a successful cross-functional project you led or participated in.

---

## Interview Closing Questions

### Candidate Questions
- What questions do you have about the role?
- What questions do you have about our company?
- Is there anything else you'd like to share that we haven't covered?

### Next Steps
- Explain the remaining steps in the process
- Discuss timeline for decision
- Ask about their timeline and other opportunities
- Request reference list (managers from each position)

### Final Assessment Questions (for interviewers)
After the interview, consider:
- Would I enthusiastically rehire this person?
- Is this person in the top 10% of talent for this role?
- Can this person achieve the accountabilities on the scorecard?
- What are the key risks with this candidate?
- What reference questions should we prioritize?
