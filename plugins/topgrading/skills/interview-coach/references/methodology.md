# Topgrading Methodology Reference

## What is Topgrading?

Topgrading is a comprehensive hiring methodology developed by Dr. Bradford Smart designed to help organizations consistently hire "A Players" - the top 10% of talent available for a given role at the compensation offered.

**Core Promise**: Organizations that properly implement Topgrading report hiring success rates of 75-90% A Players, compared to the industry average of 25%.

---

## The Six Hiring Problems (and Topgrading Solutions)

### Problem 1: Vague Job Descriptions
**Traditional Approach**: Generic job descriptions listing responsibilities and requirements
**Why It Fails**: Doesn't specify measurable outcomes; candidates can't assess fit

**Topgrading Solution**: **Job Scorecard**
- Clear mission statement
- 10-15 measurable accountabilities with specific metrics
- 15-20 competencies with Minimum Acceptable Ratings
- Compensation transparency

---

### Problem 2: Too Few Qualified Applicants
**Traditional Approach**: Post jobs, wait for applicants
**Why It Fails**: A Players are rarely looking; passive sourcing misses them

**Topgrading Solution**: **Proactive Recruiting**
- Build reputation as company that hires A Players
- Network referrals from existing A Players
- Topgrading interviews attract A Players (they appreciate rigor)
- A Players tell other A Players about positive experiences

---

### Problem 3: Candidates Fool You
**Traditional Approach**: Trust resumes and what candidates say
**Why It Fails**: Candidates present best self; hard to verify claims

**Topgrading Solution**: **TORC (Threat of Reference Check)**
- Candidates arrange their own reference calls
- References are with actual former managers (not cherry-picked friends)
- A Players are eager to have references contacted
- B/C Players self-select out or become more honest
- Creates environment of truth throughout interview

---

### Problem 4: Common Interviews Don't Reveal Negatives
**Traditional Approach**: 30-60 minute interviews, hypothetical questions
**Why It Fails**: Too short; candidates hide weaknesses; surface-level assessment

**Topgrading Solution**: **Chronological Topgrading Interview**
- 2-4 hours (comprehensive but efficient)
- Every job from education forward
- 5 core questions per job
- 18+ probing follow-up questions
- Pattern of performance becomes clear
- Weaknesses surface naturally
- Tandem interviewing for better assessment

---

### Problem 5: Poor Reference Verification
**Traditional Approach**: HR verifies dates; candidates provide friendly references
**Why It Fails**: References are pre-screened to be positive; no real insight

**Topgrading Solution**: **Candidate-Arranged Reference Calls**
- Candidate arranges calls with former managers
- If candidate can't/won't arrange call, that's telling
- Questions based on interview insights
- Verify what candidate said
- A Players' references confirm excellence
- Uncovers issues candidate minimized

---

### Problem 6: Weak Screening Tools
**Traditional Approach**: Resume screening, phone screens, assessments
**Why It Fails**: Easy to game; doesn't predict actual performance

**Topgrading Solution**: **Career History Form + PreScreen Snapshot**
- Detailed career history before interview
- Screening identifies honest vs. deceptive candidates
- Separates A Player candidates from others
- Saves interview time for qualified candidates

## TORC: The Truth Motivator

### What is TORC?
TORC stands for "Threat of Reference Check" - though it's not actually threatening. It's a methodology that creates an environment where honesty is the best strategy for candidates.

### How TORC Works

**Step 1: Set Expectations Early**
At the start of the interview, explain:
> "As part of our process, we'll ask you to arrange reference calls with your former managers. We find this gives us a complete picture and helps you present yourself accurately."

**Step 2: Apply Throughout Interview**
For each job discussed:
> "When we speak with [manager name], what do you think they'll say about your performance?"
> "What would they say was your biggest strength? Area for improvement?"

**Step 3: Follow Through**
- Actually conduct the reference calls
- Ask about specific things candidate mentioned
- Verify accomplishments and ratings

### Why TORC Works

**For A Players**:
- They welcome reference checks
- They have strong relationships with former managers
- They're confident in what will be said
- They appreciate the thorough process

**For B/C Players**:
- They become more honest (knowing it will be verified)
- They may reveal concerns proactively
- Some self-select out of the process
- Exaggerations are corrected

### TORC Psychology
- Creates cognitive dissonance when candidates consider lying
- Knowing verification is coming, honesty becomes easier
- A Players see it as an advantage (they have great references)
- Process becomes self-selecting for integrity

## The Topgrading Interview

### Format: Chronological Career History
Unlike traditional interviews that jump around topics, Topgrading follows the candidate's career from beginning to end.

**Why Chronological?**
- Patterns emerge over time
- Can't hide gaps or issues
- Context builds naturally
- Stories connect logically
- Harder to fabricate consistently

### The Five Core Questions (Per Job)
1. What were you hired to do?
2. What accomplishments are you most proud of?
3. What were some low points during that job?
4. Who were the people you worked with?
5. Why did you leave that job?

### Tandem Interviewing
Two interviewers working together:
- **Primary Interviewer**: Asks questions, builds rapport, probes
- **Note-taker**: Documents responses, tracks patterns, prepares follow-ups

**Benefits**:
- More complete documentation
- Different perspectives
- Reduced bias
- Better candidate experience (no note-taking pauses)
- Calibration between interviewers

## A Player Definition

### The Standard
**An A Player is someone in the top 10% of talent available for the role at the compensation offered.**

### Characteristics of A Players
1. **Results-Oriented**: Consistently achieves or exceeds goals
2. **Self-Motivated**: Drives own performance without constant supervision
3. **Integrity**: Honest, ethical, trustworthy
4. **Team Player**: Makes others around them better
5. **Coachable**: Open to feedback and continuous improvement
6. **Accountable**: Owns outcomes, doesn't make excuses
7. **Adaptable**: Performs well in changing conditions

### The Rehire Test
**Key Question**: Would you enthusiastically rehire this person?
- "Yes, absolutely" = A Player
- "Probably" or "With some reservations" = B Player
- "No" = C Player

## Cost of Mis-Hire

### Why This Matters
Understanding the cost of a bad hire creates urgency around doing hiring right.

### Cost Formula
Total cost includes:
1. **Hiring Costs**: Recruiting fees, advertising, interview time, assessments
2. **Compensation**: Salary, benefits, bonuses paid during tenure
3. **Training & Onboarding**: Time and resources to get them productive
4. **Management Time**: Supervision, coaching, performance management
5. **Mistakes & Failures**: Errors, rework, missed opportunities
6. **Disruption**: Team morale, customer relationships, momentum
7. **Severance**: Exit package, unemployment, legal costs
8. **Replacement**: Starting over with hiring process

### Industry Benchmarks

| Role Level | Cost Multiple | Example (on $100K salary) |
|------------|--------------|---------------------------|
| Entry | 1-2x base | $100K - $200K |
| Professional | 3-5x base | $300K - $500K |
| Manager | 5-8x base | $500K - $800K |
| Director | 8-15x base | $800K - $1.5M |
| VP | 10-20x base | $1M - $2M |
| Executive | 15-27x base | $1.5M - $2.7M |

### The Math
If you hire 10 people and 7 are B/C Players (industry average), and each costs $200K to replace, that's $1.4M in hiring failures.

With Topgrading (75%+ A Players), you might have only 2-3 mis-hires = $400-600K. **Savings: $800K-$1M**.

## Interviewer Skills

### Essential Behaviors
1. **Build Rapport**: Make candidate comfortable to get honest responses
2. **Control Pace**: Keep interview on track while allowing depth
3. **Probe Effectively**: Ask follow-up questions to get specifics
4. **Listen Actively**: Hear what's said and not said
5. **Take Notes**: Document specific examples and evidence
6. **Stay Neutral**: Don't telegraph right/wrong answers
7. **Manage Time**: Cover all ground within time available

### Common Mistakes
1. **Talking too much**: Should be 80/20 candidate to interviewer
2. **Leading questions**: "You're good at teamwork, right?"
3. **Accepting vague answers**: "I'm a hard worker"
4. **Skipping TORC**: Not asking about manager references
5. **Rushing**: Not giving time for complete answers
6. **Hypotheticals only**: "What would you do if..." instead of "Tell me about a time when..."
7. **Confirmation bias**: Looking for evidence of first impression

### The 24-Point Interviewer Feedback Form
Rate interviewers on:
- Initial rapport building (5 items)
- Throughout interview behaviors (20 items)
- Interview probe effectiveness (7 items)

Use for training and continuous improvement.
